# Product and service offers

These are offers that can be sold after a payment account is configured. They
are not active checkout links and do not represent earned income.

## Verified bounty digest: EUR 9 per month

Send a small weekly list of opportunities whose GitHub issues are genuinely open,
whose repositories are active, and whose payout limitations are disclosed.

- Starter: EUR 9 per month for up to 20 verified opportunities.
- Team: EUR 29 per month for up to 100 opportunities and a JSON export.
- Ten starter subscribers would produce EUR 90 in gross monthly revenue before
  processing costs and applicable tax.

## Repository bounty audit: EUR 49 fixed price

Review up to 25 advertised rewards for a project, community, or engineering
team. Separate active issues, stale listings, unsupported payout claims,
excessive competition, and contributor-policy conflicts.

- Up to 25 listings: EUR 49.
- Up to 100 listings: EUR 149.
- Custom integration: from EUR 299 after agreeing scope in writing.

## White-label preflight endpoint: EUR 79 per month

Offer a hosted endpoint that accepts a GitHub issue URL plus listing metadata
and returns an evidence-backed risk assessment as JSON.

- Beta: EUR 79 per month for up to 1,000 checks.
- The customer supplies its own GitHub token.
- Never claim that a reward is escrowed without independent evidence.
- Deployment, billing, and customer onboarding are not yet configured.
